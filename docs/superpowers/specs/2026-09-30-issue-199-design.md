# Issue #199 design: square 7×10 chapter grid with tag and bookmark markers

Research: `docs/superpowers/research/2026-09-30-issue-199-research.md` (read at `67eaaf1e`).
Surface: `ui`. Tier: `standard`.

Modelled on:
- `7186b796` (#15), which introduced the number grid: `NumberGridLayout.h`, `test/number_grid/`,
  the cell cap and the `MAX_INTERACTIONS` rationale. This change revises that change set.
- `src/activities/reader/BibleEntryPosition.h` + `test/number_grid/BibleEntryPositionTest.cpp`
  for the new pure marker-mapping header and its host test.
- `key-grid.h:85-91` (the Space glyph, drawn after `button()` with the key's state-resolved ink)
  for drawing a marker that stays visible on the inverted cell.
- `BibleSearchIndexer::logMemory` (`src/study/BibleSearchIndexer.cpp:321-327`) for the heap log.

## 1. Problem

- The chapter and verse grids hold 42 cells per page in portrait. `geometryFor` caps rows at
  `MAX_CELLS / cols` = 48 / 7 = 6 (`src/activities/reader/NumberGridLayout.h:19,39`), so
  Genesis (50) and Isaiah (66) take 2 pages and Psalms (150) takes 4.
- Cells are stretched slabs, not squares: `buildGrid` hands `keyGrid` the whole body
  (`BibleNavigationActivity.cpp:489`), and `keyGrid` sizes width and height independently
  (`freeink-sdk/libs/ui/FreeInkUI/include/components/keyboard/key-grid.h:54-55`).
- Nothing on the grid shows where the user has something. Tagged passages and bookmarks are both
  resident while the reader is open (`StudyStore.h:88`; `EpubReaderActivity.h:43`), but the
  navigator receives only `epub` and the entry spine (`BibleNavigationActivity.cpp:33-37`).

## 2. Goal

1. Chapter and verse grids are 7 columns × up to 10 rows of **square** cells, centred in the body.
   Genesis and Isaiah fit on one page; Psalms takes 3.
2. A chapter cell whose chapter holds one of the user's tagged passages shows a dot; one holding a
   bookmark shows a bookmark mark. The same at the verse level, per verse. The current chapter
   stays inverted, and its markers stay visible on it.
3. Nothing new is stored. Marker sets are computed when a level is loaded.

## 3. Non-goals

- The book-level "has something" dot (issue item 3, optional) — deferred; see A11.
- Any change to the book grid's layout (A3).
- The mockup's legend line and cover masthead (the masthead is overhaul phase 6).
- Reading progress of any kind (issue rule; memory "Bible is reference, not progress").
- Changing `ReaderBookmarks` behaviour, the bookmark file, `StudyStore` or any on-disk format.
- `freeink-sdk` changes.

## 4. Assumptions (each is a decision the review should attack)

**A1 — Caps.** `NumberGrid::MAX_CELLS` 48 → 70 and `UiAppHost::MAX_INTERACTIONS` 64 → 96, as the
issue specifies. 96 leaves 26 slots over a full 70-cell page for anything else in the frame. The
navigator's header is painted by `GUI.drawHeader` (`BibleNavigationActivity.cpp:553-555`), not as
FreeInkUI interactions, so 70 is the page's whole count today; the margin is for future chrome.
Cost: +32 slots × 32 B = **+1,024 B per live `UiAppHost`** (`UiAppHost.h:31-34`), for every FUI
screen, not just this one. The `UiAppHost.h:29-34` comment is updated to name the 70-cell page
and the new per-host cost.

**A2 — Geometry is unchanged apart from the cap.** `geometryFor` keeps its formula
(`NumberGridLayout.h:34-41`). With stride 64, any body ≥ 440 wide gets 7 columns and any body
≥ 632 tall gets 10 rows once the cap is 70. The current Lyra chapter body is computed at about
480 × 743 (research §2; not measured on device), and the old test fixture 480 × 695 also yields
7 × 10. Both keep cells ≥ `MIN_CELL` 56 because rows and columns are only counted where a full
56 px + gap fits.

**A3 — The book grid keeps its 48-cell cap.** `BookGridLayout.h:91` reads `NumberGrid::MAX_CELLS`.
Left alone, the raise changes book-level rows wherever the cap binds (e.g. six columns on a
body tall enough for 11 rows gives 6 × 11 instead of 6 × 8; `CellCapLimitsRowsWhenManyColumnsFit`,
`BookGridLayoutTest.cpp:77-85`), a visible layout change the issue does not ask for. A new
`BookGrid::MAX_CELLS = 48` replaces that reference, and the three `BookGridLayoutTest.cpp`
assertions against `NumberGrid::MAX_CELLS` (lines 73, 79-84) move to `BookGrid::MAX_CELLS`,
leaving every expected value as it is today.

**A4 — Square, centred cells are computed in the activity, not the SDK.** A new pure
`NumberGrid::gridRect(bodyW, bodyH, geometry, gap)` returns the offset and size of a rect exactly
`cols*cell + (cols-1)*gap` wide and `rows*cell + (rows-1)*gap` tall, with `cell =
cellSizeFor(...)` (`NumberGridLayout.h:44-49`), centred on **both** axes of the body (integer
division; any odd pixel goes to the right/bottom). `buildGrid` passes that rect to `keyGrid`,
whose own arithmetic then produces `cellW == cellH == cell`. `minTouchSize` stays
`cellSizeFor(...)` (`BibleNavigationActivity.cpp:482-485`) so hit rects never overlap. Padding
cells on the last page keep their space (the grid stays rectangular, `.cpp:463-468`), so a short
final page is not re-centred.

**A5 — Page counts follow from A1-A2.** 70 per page: Genesis 1 page, Isaiah 1, Psalms 3
(150 → 70 + 70 + 10), Psalm 119's verses 3 (176 → 70 + 70 + 36).

**A6 — Marker sets are per level, not one whole-Bible bitset.** The issue suggests a 1,189-bit
(149 B) set; the navigator only ever shows one book's chapters or one chapter's verses, and a
whole-Bible set would still need per-book offsets to index. Instead the activity holds two
fixed bitsets per level, sized to the largest list:
`GridMarks::Bits` with `CAPACITY = 176` (Psalm 119, the longest verse list; ≥ `MAX_CHAPTERS` 150,
`BibleNavigationActivity.h:40`) = 22 B each. Four of them (tag/bookmark × chapter/verse) = 88 B,
by value inside the activity object. No heap allocation. A cell index ≥ `CAPACITY` is never
marked (logged once per load at `LOG_DBG`), never out of bounds.

**A7 — Which task builds the marks.** Chapter marks are built at the end of `loadChapters()`
(`.cpp:112-140`) and verse marks at the end of `loadVerses()` (`.cpp:142-155`), on the loop task,
immediately after the data they index (`chapterSpine`, `verseAnchors`) is written, and are read
by `buildGrid` on the render task. This is the same hand-off `chapterSpine` and `verseAnchors`
already use: written by `load*()` before `enterLevel()` publishes the level switch under
`RenderLock` (`.cpp:218-234`). Clearing then filling a level's bits happens while that level is
not the one on screen (the loads are reached from `activateIndex`/`enterAtPosition` before
`enterLevel`), so the render task never reads half-built bits for the level it is drawing.

**A8 — Tagged passage → cells.** Read `STUDY.passages()` (`StudyStore.h:88`) only when
`STUDY.isOpen()` (`:90`); otherwise no tag marks. Only passages whose `start` is a `Verse` unit
(`Unit.h:23-29`) are considered; `Paragraph` and `DocumentOffset` passages have `book = 0` and
cannot name a chapter.
- A passage's span is `start` to `end` when `end` is also a `Verse` unit of the same book and
  not before `start` (by `(major, minor)`); otherwise the span is `start` alone.
- **Chapter level:** mark every chapter row `r` with `start.major <= r + 1 <= end.major` in the
  book `selectedBook + 1`. Navigator book `i` ↔ `Unit.book == i + 1` because both number books by
  position in biblebooknav.xhtml (`Unit.h:9-15`; `loadBooks`, `.cpp:65-72`). Chapter row `r` ↔
  chapter `r + 1` (`cellLabel`, `.cpp:531-532`).
- **Verse level:** mark verse cell `i` when `(verseAnchors[i].chapter, verseAnchors[i].verse)`
  (`VerseAnchors.h:19-23`) lies within `[(start.major, start.minor), (end.major, end.minor)]`
  and the book matches. The comparison uses the anchor's own chapter, so a single-chapter book
  (reached with `selectedChapterRow = -1`, `.cpp:246-255`) works without special casing.
- Rationale for marking the whole span: a passage spanning chapters 5-6 "holds" something in
  both; showing it only on 5 would hide it when the user browses to 6.

**A9 — Bookmark → cells.** The navigator receives a copy of each bookmark's
`computedSpineIndex`, `hasVisibleTextOffset` and `visibleTextOffset`
(`BookmarkEntry.h:11,18-19`), not a pointer into the reader:
- `ReaderBookmarks` gains `const std::vector<BookmarkEntry>& entries() const` next to `empty()`
  (`ReaderBookmarks.h:39`).
- The navigator constructor gains `const std::vector<BookmarkEntry>& bookmarks`;
  `EpubReaderActivity.cpp:724` passes `bookmarks.entries()`. The constructor copies the three
  fields into a member `std::vector<GridMarks::BookmarkPosition>` (8 B each).
- Why a copy: the reader stays on `stackActivities` while the navigator runs
  (`ActivityManager.cpp:115-117`), but a copy removes any lifetime coupling for a few hundred
  bytes.
- Allocation: one `makeUniqueNoThrow<GridMarks::BookmarkPosition[]>(n)` plus a count, once per
  navigator open, freed with the activity. Not a `std::vector`, whose growth aborts on OOM under
  `-fno-exceptions`; OOM logs `LOG_ERR` and leaves the navigator with no bookmark marks. A static
  array was rejected because the count has no small fixed bound: n is the open book's bookmark
  count, limited only indirectly by the bookmark file's byte budget
  (`BookmarkDoc::SAVE_BYTE_BUDGET`, `src/util/BookmarkFile.cpp:56-57`). At typical counts
  (< 100 → < 800 B, below the 4,096 B PSRAM routing threshold, research §2) it lands in internal
  SRAM.
- **Chapter level:** a bookmark marks row `r` where `chapterSpine[r] == computedSpineIndex`
  (the lookup `BibleEntry::chapterRowFor` already performs, `BibleEntryPosition.h`; reused, not
  duplicated).
- **Verse level:** a bookmark with `computedSpineIndex == verseSpine` and
  `hasVisibleTextOffset` marks the last verse cell whose anchor `offset <= visibleTextOffset`.
  Both offsets are visible-codepoint offsets the reader consumes as `offsetJump`
  (`EpubReaderActivity.cpp:679-683` for a picked bookmark, `:735` for the navigator's verse).
  A bookmark before the first anchor marks verse cell 0. A pre-offset bookmark marks its chapter
  but no verse.

**A10 — What the markers look like.**
- Placement: the top-right corner of the cell, as in the mockup and as `secondaryLabel` places
  it (`key-grid.h:93-96`: right third, top half). The bookmark mark sits at the right edge, the
  dot immediately to its left, so both can show on one cell (mockup order "•⌂").
- **Dot:** U+2022 `•`, drawn as text in the theme's `smallText` style. It is present in every
  Ubuntu UI and Noto built-in font (research §4.3).
- **Bookmark:** the existing 16 × 16 `BookmarkStatusIcon` ribbon (`src/components/icons/bookmark.h:15`)
  drawn as a `BitmapRef` (`BitmapFormat::BW1`, set bit = ink, `FreeInkUICore.h:350-355`,
  `FreeInkUIGfxRenderer.h:197-209`). **This replaces the issue's `⌂`**: U+2302 is in none of the
  built-in fonts (research §4.3), and the ribbon is the mark the reader's status bar already uses
  for a bookmarked page (`BaseTheme.cpp:34-41,678`), so the grid and the reader agree.
- **Ink follows the cell's state**, mirroring the Space glyph (`key-grid.h:85-91`): black on a
  normal cell, white on the selected (inverted) cell, since the key style fills the selected cell
  black with white foreground (`FreeInkUI.cpp:25-26`). `TextStyle.color`
  (`FreeInkUICore.h:537`) and `bitmap(..., foreground)` (`FreeInkUICore.h:708-710`) both take
  the ink. `secondaryLabel` is **not** used because its style is fixed across states and would
  draw black on the black selected cell (research §4.2).
- Drawing: after `fui::keyGrid(...)` returns, `buildGrid` walks the page's marked cells and
  draws into `screen.frame().target()`, using a pure `NumberGrid::cellRect(grid rect, cell, gap,
  cols, index)` for each cell's rect. Drawing directly on the target after a component is how
  `UiSliderDialog.h:57-60` draws its +/- glyphs.
- Padding (disabled) cells are never marked.

**A11 — No book-level dot.** Optional in the issue. Book cells are sized to abbreviations
(`BookGridLayout.h:46-50`), not square, and a per-book "has something" set needs every book's
chapter spines, which the navigator only resolves for the open book (`loadChapters`). Deferred to
its own issue rather than adding a 66-document scan to navigator entry.

**A12 — Refresh.** No new refresh behaviour. Markers are part of the same frame the grid already
paints; paging and selection moves keep today's refresh path.

## 5. Architecture

### New: `src/activities/reader/GridMarks.h`
Pure header, free of FreeInkUI, Arduino and Epub parsing, modelled on `BibleEntryPosition.h`.
Includes `<StudyStore/Unit.h>` (std-only, `Unit.h:1-5`) and `<Epub/VerseAnchors.h>` for the
`VerseAnchor` struct only (`VerseAnchors.h:1-23`, std-only includes; no `.cpp` needed for the
struct).

```cpp
namespace GridMarks {
constexpr int CAPACITY = 176;

class Bits {  // fixed, no heap
 public:
  void clear();
  void set(int index);            // ignores index outside [0, CAPACITY)
  bool test(int index) const;     // false outside [0, CAPACITY)
 private:
  uint8_t bytes[(CAPACITY + 7) / 8] = {};
};

struct BookmarkPosition { uint16_t spine; bool hasOffset; uint32_t offset; };

// A8: the verse span a passage covers in `book` (1-66), or false when it names no verse.
struct VerseSpan { uint16_t startChapter, startVerse, endChapter, endVerse; };
bool spanFor(const study::Unit& start, const study::Unit& end, uint8_t book, VerseSpan& out);

void markPassageChapters(Bits&, const VerseSpan&, int chapterCount);
void markPassageVerses(Bits&, const VerseSpan&, const VerseAnchors::VerseAnchor* anchors, int count);
void markBookmarkChapters(Bits&, const BookmarkPosition*, int n, const int16_t* chapterSpine, int chapterCount);
void markBookmarkVerses(Bits&, const BookmarkPosition*, int n, int verseSpine,
                        const VerseAnchors::VerseAnchor* anchors, int count);
}
```

Exact signatures are the plan's to settle; the contract is: plain inputs, no singletons, every
index bounds-checked.

## 6. Data and control flow

```
Reader menu → SELECT_CHAPTER (EpubReaderActivity.cpp:710)
  └─ new BibleNavigationActivity(..., epub, spineIdx, bookmarks.entries())
       ctor: copy bookmark positions (makeUniqueNoThrow; OOM → count 0, LOG_ERR)
  onEnter (loop task)
    loadBooks()
    enterAtPosition() → loadChapters(book) → build chapterTagged / chapterBookmarked
                      → enterLevel(Chapter, row)            [RenderLock]
    log memory
  render task: buildScreen → buildGrid
    geometryFor → 7×10; gridRect → centred square rect
    keyGrid(rect) → 70 buttons, current cell inverted
    for each real cell on page: draw • / bookmark with state ink
  tap chapter (loop task) → activateIndex → openVerseList → loadVerses(spine)
    → build verseTagged / verseBookmarked → enterLevel(Verse, 0)
  tap verse → finishWith(verseSpine, anchor.offset)   (unchanged)
  Back from verse → enterLevel(Chapter, selectedChapterRow)  (chapter bits still valid)
  Back from chapter → book level; choosing another book → loadChapters rebuilds chapter bits
```

## 7. Error handling

| Condition | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `STUDY.isOpen()` false | No tag marks; grid otherwise normal. |
| Bookmark copy OOM | `LOG_ERR("BNV", "OOM: %d bookmark positions")`; no bookmark marks. |
| Passage with a non-Verse start, other book, or reversed span | Start-only or ignored per A8; never an error. |
| Bookmark spine not in the open book / chapter | Not marked. |
| Index ≥ `GridMarks::CAPACITY` | Not marked; one `LOG_DBG` per load. |
| `loadChapters` / `loadVerses` fails | That level's bits cleared (existing fallbacks unchanged). |
| Interaction overflow | Still logged by `UiAppHost.cpp:23-25`; A1 keeps 70 < 96. |

No path writes to SD. Marker failures never block navigation.

## 8. Memory

| Item | Size | Where |
|---|---|---|
| `MAX_INTERACTIONS` 64 → 96 | +1,024 B per live `UiAppHost` | inside each FUI activity object; PSRAM when the object exceeds 4,096 B, else internal SRAM (research §2) |
| `cells[]` 48 → 70 `KeyGridKey` + `cellLabels` 48×4 → 70×4 | +22 × (sizeof(KeyGridKey) + 4) B | navigator object |
| Four `GridMarks::Bits` | 88 B | navigator object |
| Bookmark positions | 8 B × bookmark count | heap, freed with the navigator |

The navigator object is already over 4,096 B (`bookNames` alone is 66 × 48 B,
`BibleBookNameTable.h:21,25,41`), so its growth is expected in PSRAM; the logged internal-free
figure (§5) is how the device test confirms it.

## 9. Testing strategy

TDD, host suite in `test/number_grid/` (surface-local `CMakeLists.txt`; no edit to the shared
`test/CMakeLists.txt`).

**`NumberGridLayoutTest.cpp` (extend):**
- Portrait 480 × 743 (current computed body) and 480 × 695 (old fixture) both give 7 × 10 and
  `cellsPerPage() == 70 <= MAX_CELLS`.
- Any height in [632, 2000] at width 480 gives 7 columns and ≤ 10 rows; `cellSizeFor >= MIN_CELL`.
- Paging: `pageCount(50, 70) == 1`, `pageCount(66, 70) == 1`, `pageCount(150, 70) == 3`,
  `pageCount(176, 70) == 3`; last Psalms page holds 10 cells.
- `gridRect`: square (`cellW == cellH == cellSizeFor`) and centred within the body on both axes;
  fits inside the body; degenerate geometry returns an empty rect.
- `cellRect`: first, last-in-row, first-of-last-row, and last cell of a full 7 × 10 page land
  inside `gridRect` and match what `keyGrid`'s formula (`key-grid.h:54-61`) produces for that
  rect — the edge cells the device tap test checks.
- Existing `Psalm119TakesFourPages` (48 per page) is rewritten for 70 per page.

**`BookGridLayoutTest.cpp`:** assertions move to `BookGrid::MAX_CELLS`; every expected value is
unchanged — proving A3.

**New `GridMarksTest.cpp` (+ `add_executable` in `test/number_grid/CMakeLists.txt`):**
- `Bits`: set/test, clear, out-of-range set ignored and test false.
- Passages: single verse marks one chapter; a chapter-spanning passage marks each chapter in
  range; another book marks nothing; a non-Verse unit marks nothing; a Verse start with a
  non-Verse or reversed end marks the start only; verse level marks exactly the verses in range,
  including a span entering from the previous chapter; single-chapter book.
- Bookmarks: chapter spine match marks the row; spine outside the book marks nothing; verse
  offset before the first anchor → cell 0, between anchors → the earlier, past the last → the
  last; pre-offset bookmark marks no verse; different spine marks no verse.
- Fixtures are spine numbers, chapter counts and synthetic offsets only — no publisher text
  (memory: public-repo fixtures).

**Build:** `pio run` (x4pro) once after the last edit; `./bin/clang-format-fix` over the tree.

**Device (human):**
- Isaiah and Genesis on one page; Psalms 3 pages; cells square and centred.
- Tap the four corner cells and the middle of the edges of a full 7 × 10 page (e.g. Psalms p.1):
  each opens its own chapter.
- A chapter with a tagged passage shows •; one with a bookmark shows the ribbon; both show on
  the current (inverted) chapter in white.
- Serial: the `BNV` memory line with the grid open, compared against `main` — internal free flat
  apart from the documented host growth.
