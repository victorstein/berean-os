# Issue #203 design: Home redesign for a reference Bible

Status: draft for spec review. Research: `docs/superpowers/research/2026-09-30-issue-203-research.md`.
Base: `75c65e93`. The owner's mockup is artifact `4qvfHbNnJ77F2gM5DELQrQ` ("homeProposed").

## Problem

Home (`src/activities/launcher/LauncherActivity.cpp`) is a shelf of four tiles and a resume strip.

- The Bible tile opens the book where it was left (`openBible`, 429-433). There is no way to get
  back to a *place*, to the book grid, to search or to tags without first entering the reader and
  its menu.
- The resume strip resumes whatever book was opened last (`recents[0]`, 76-80). It knows nothing
  about places.

Issue #201 made places (`PLACES`, `src/PlacesStore.h:42`) and entry intents
(`ReaderEntryIntent`, `src/activities/reader/ReaderEntryIntent.h`) available, but nothing produces
an intent yet: every `goToReader(` caller passes the defaults (research, "Places and intents").

## Goal

Home per Direction B v2. Getting back to a place takes one tap, and Home shows no Bible progress.

- **Cover hero.** The Bible cover through `CoverBand`. Its plate carries **Continue · <newest
  place>** and **Go to…**.
- **Recent places.** The next places, up to three rows. Each opens there (`OpenAt`).
- **From your tags.** A whole tagged Bible passage, picked with the sleep picker's machinery. It is
  painted after the rest of Home and opens its passage (`OpenAt`).
- **Meetings card.** A compact week strip (`buildWeekStrip`) with the meeting days from Settings,
  and the Workbook weekly %.
- **Icon row.** Tags, Search, Publications and Settings.

## Non-goals

- No change to the places store, the intent contract, or how the reader consumes intents (#201
  owns them).
- No change to `CoverBand`, `Masthead`, or what the sleep screen shows or records.
- No new stored data: no new file under `/.berean/`, and no new key in `state.json`. See A6 for
  the one behaviour this rules out.
- No reading progress for the Bible anywhere on Home.
- Tags, Search and Go to open inside the Bible only. Tags for other publications stay in their
  reader.
- No new icon art.

## Assumptions (each one is a decision for review to attack)

- **A1 — Continue is the newest place, and the "resume any book" strip goes.**
  - Continue is `goToReader(biblePath, true, ReaderEntryIntent::openAt(PLACES.getPlaces()[0]))`.
  - The old strip resumed `recents[0]` of any kind (`LauncherActivity.cpp:76-80, 421-422`). The
    issue defines Continue as "the newest place from the places store", and places exist only for
    the Bible (`ReaderEntryIntent.h:35`), so a Watchtower or other book is no longer one tap from
    Home.
    - This week's meeting publications are two taps away, through the Meetings card.
    - Anything else is reached through Publications.
- **A2 — No places yet.** When `PLACES.getPlaces()` is empty, the button reads
  `tr(STR_CONTINUE_READING)` (`english.yaml:16`) and calls `goToReader(biblePath, true)`. That
  opens the Bible at its saved position, which is today's Bible tile behaviour
  (`LauncherActivity.cpp:431`).
- **A3 — Recent rows.** `PlacesDoc::pickRecent(places, places[0].unit, out, 3)`
  (`src/util/PlacesDoc.h:67-69`, `PlacesDoc.cpp:78-87`).
  - Places are unique per (book, chapter) (`PlacesDoc.h:78-79`), so skipping the head's chapter
    skips exactly the head.
  - The section keeps three fixed row slots whatever the count, so Home's layout never moves
    between entries. Empty slots are blank and untappable.
  - The section label is `tr(STR_RECENT)` (`english.yaml:481`).
- **A4 — The verse card reads the Bible's passages only.**
  - It reads `/.berean/passages/bible.json`, which is `PASSAGES_DIR`
    (`lib/Serialization/SdPaths.h:31`) plus `study::BIBLE_PUB_KEY`
    (`lib/StudyStore/StudyStore/PubKey.h`).
  - OpenAt resolves only in the Bible (`ReaderEntryIntent.h:36-39`). A pick from another
    publication would be a card whose tap does nothing useful.
  - Scanning one file is also the tightest possible bound, `MAX_FILE_BYTES = SAVE_BYTE_BUDGET + 4096`
    (`StudySleepScreen.cpp:42`, `PassageDoc.h:38`).
- **A5 — The pick is the sleep picker's, with a card-sized fit gate.**
  - Rows are filtered exactly as `offerRow` does: addressable start, whole text, within the
    prefilter, and fits (`StudySleepScreen.cpp:189-223`).
  - Sampling is `study_sleep::Sampler` (`StudySleepPick.h:103-148`).
  - Fit is decided before sampling, as on the sleep screen (`StudySleepPick.h:98-102`), against
    the **card's** text box. The rungs are serif 14 italic, then serif 12 italic, then Ubuntu 10
    regular, taken from `PASSAGE_FONT_IDS`/`PASSAGE_FONT_STYLES` (`StudySleepScreen.cpp:61-64`).
    A passage that does not fit the card whole is never shown cut, as #188 requires of the sleep
    screen.
- **A6 — "Once per day": the day seeds the pick, and RAM caches it for the wake.** **Decided
  (d1): see "Decision record".**
  - Deep sleep is a full chip reset (`src/main.cpp:140-143`), so nothing in RAM survives from one
    wake to the next, and the device sleeps many times a day. A cache that is only in RAM would
    re-pick on the first Home of every wake and show a different verse each time.
  - Decided:
    - The sampler's `RandomFn` (`StudySleepPick.h:88`) is a small PRNG seeded from the local
      date's `yyyymmdd`. One file, read in file order, with the same seed gives the same pick all
      day for as long as the passages are unchanged. Nothing is stored.
    - The result is cached in RAM for the wake, keyed on the date, so later Home entries in the
      same wake don't scan. Each wake pays one scan on its first Home entry and paints the same
      verse. The scan's duration is logged once per wake, on that one scan.
  - The recent-sleep ring (`CrossPointState.h:31-33`) is neither read nor written, and
    `APP_STATE.pushRecentStudySleep` (`CrossPointState.h:53`) is never called from Home. The two
    pickers stay independent: reading the ring would make the day's pick change whenever the sleep
    screen ran.
  - Adding or removing tags during the day can change the pick, because the sampler sees a
    different set of rows. That is accepted (d1).
- **A7 — No clock.** When `readLocalDate` fails (`src/util/LocalDate.h:8`), the seed is a fixed
  fallback constant, `HOME_VERSE_FALLBACK_SEED`, logged at `LOG_DBG` (d1). It is never hardware
  random. The pick is cached for the wake under a "no date" key, so it neither crashes nor thrashes,
  and it stays the same across wakes until the clock is set.
- **A8 — The cache is a RAM singleton.** It is modelled on `CatalogIndexStore::getInstance()`
  (`src/network/CatalogIndexStore.cpp:36-39`), a non-persistent singleton whose state (its stamp)
  outlives the screen that loaded it (`CatalogIndexStore.h`, `release()`).
  - It holds `{bool valid; bool dated; CivilDate day; study_sleep::Candidate pick;}`.
  - `Candidate::text` is bounded by `FIT_PREFILTER_BYTES = 4096` (`StudySleepPick.h:29`), and in
    practice by what fits the card, a few hundred bytes.
  - It is internal-SRAM heap, since it is under the 4096-byte PSRAM auto-routing threshold. That
    is accepted because it is small, lives for the wake, and is replaced at most once a day, so it
    does not fragment.
- **A9 — A cached pick can go stale within the wake.** Deleting the passage in the reader does not
  update the cache. Tapping the card still opens its verse, because the address is the unit, not
  the row. The next wake re-scans and drops it. The same
  applies to tags added mid-day, which may change the next wake's pick (A6). Accepted rather than adding a mutation counter to
  `STUDY`, which is another surface.
- **A10 — Paint first, then fill.** This mirrors `MeetingsActivity::loop`
  (`src/activities/network/MeetingsActivity.cpp:285-293`).
  - On a cache miss, `onEnter` sets `versePending`, and the first `render` draws the card with its
    label and an empty body.
  - `loop()` then sees the flag, calls `requestUpdateAndWait()` so Home is on the panel, runs the
    scan, fills the cache and calls `requestUpdate()`.
  - The scan runs on the loop task, not in a new FreeRTOS task. A task would share the renderer's
    font measurement (`measurePassage`, `StudySleepScreen.cpp:133-136`) with the render task,
    which nothing here does today.
  - The cost is that input is not polled during the scan, so a tap that starts and ends inside it
    can be missed. The scan is bounded by A4 and timed in the log, and the verification step
    below judges whether it is short enough.
- **A11 — The card when there is nothing to show.** If there is no `bible.json`, or no row is whole
  and fits, the card shows its label and a one-line hint, `STR_HOME_TAGS_EMPTY` ("Passages you tag
  appear here"). The card is not tappable. Its slot stays, per A3's reasoning.
- **A12 — Tapping the card** calls
  `goToReader(biblePath, false, intent{kind = OpenAt, unit = pick.start, spineHint = 0})`.
  - `Candidate` gains `study::Unit start`, parsed from the row's `"u"` (`StudySleepScreen.cpp:191`
    already validates it with `study::unitFromCompact`).
  - `spineHint = 0` sends `STUDY.locatePlace` straight to its book search
    (`EpubReaderActivity.cpp:945-954`), because a passage row stores no spine.
  - `StudySleepPick.h` stays Arduino-free: `StudyStore/Unit.h` is already included by the
    host-tested `ReaderEntryIntent.h`.
- **A13 — The Meetings card.**
  - A tap pushes `MeetingsActivity`, as the Meetings tile does today
    (`LauncherActivity.cpp:448-451`).
  - The cover is this week's publication, found as today (`thisWeeksMeetingPublication`, 148-167,
    then the registry, then the card scan). It is drawn as a small cover at the card's height
    through `CoverThumb::pathFor`, as `MeetingsActivity::fillCard` does (`MeetingsActivity.cpp:253`).
    Its thumbnail is generated once and then cached.
  - The week range comes from `formatWeekRange` (`MeetingWeekView.h:31-32`) for the **current**
    ISO week.
  - The strip is `buildWeekStrip(monday, &today, SETTINGS.midweekMeetingDay,
    SETTINGS.weekendMeetingDay)` (`MeetingWeekView.h:25`). Initials come from `copyInitial` over
    `WEEKDAY_NAME_IDS` (`src/util/WeekdayNames.h:10`), as `buildWeekHeader` does
    (`MeetingsActivity.cpp:210-215`).
  - The % is `readBookProgressPercent(workbookPath)` formatted with `STR_MEETING_PROGRESS`
    (`MeetingsActivity.cpp:274-278`). The workbook path is
    `MeetingLibrary::findPublication(MeetingPub::Workbook, entry->workbook)`. That is a separate
    lookup because `thisWeeksMeetingPublication` prefers the Watchtower (161-165).
  - With no clock, the strip and range are omitted and no day is marked, matching Meetings'
    "no local time, no marked day" rule (`MeetingsActivity.cpp:208-210`). With no workbook, there
    is no % line.
- **A14 — The icon row.**
  - **Tags**: `goToReader(biblePath, false, ReaderEntryIntent::of(Kind::Tags))`, with
    `BookmarkIcon` (`src/components/icons/bookmark.h`) because no tag icon exists.
  - **Search**: `of(Kind::Search)`, with `SearchIcon`.
  - **Publications**: pushes `PublicationsActivity` and re-resolves on return, as today
    (453-459). It uses `LibraryIcon`.
  - **Settings**: `activityManager.goToSettings()` (461), with `Settings2Icon`.
  - Labels: `STR_TAGS` (375), `STR_SEARCH` (288), `STR_PUBLICATIONS` (434),
    `STR_SETTINGS_TITLE` (14).
  - **Go to…**: `of(Kind::BookGrid)`, labelled `STR_GO_TO` (477).
- **A15 — No Bible on the card** (`biblePath` empty after `resolveBible`, 90-103).
  - The hero shows the plate's title and a single `tr(STR_DOWNLOAD)` button (`english.yaml:223`)
    that runs today's `openBible()` download path (434-445).
  - Tags and Search also route there.
  - The Recent rows and the verse card render empty, and nothing is scanned, since there is
    nothing to open.
- **A16 — Header and date.**
  - There is no separate header. The hero's plate carries the theme header, drawn with
    `GUI.drawHeader(renderer, plateHeaderRect, tr(STR_BIBLE), dateLine)`, as
    `Masthead::draw` does (`src/components/Masthead.cpp`, `draw`). That keeps the battery,
    including the #185 charging state, inside the theme.
  - `dateLine` is `formatDateLine` (`StudySleepPick.h:179-199`). `drawHeader` right-aligns the
    subtitle beside the battery (`BaseTheme.cpp:321-333`). With no clock it is null.
  - If the cover cannot be drawn, the header goes in its usual place instead
    (`Masthead.cpp` fallback). No text is ever drawn over dither.
- **A17 — Hero geometry reuses the Masthead band.**
  - `MastheadLayout::band(screenW, marginTop, marginRight, marginLeft, topPadding, heroHeight)`
    (`src/components/MastheadLayout.h:23-28`) gives x = 8 and width = 464 under Lyra.
    `thumbHeightFor(464, h) = 773` for any h ≤ 773 (`CoverBandGeometry.h:46-48`), so the hero
    asks for the `thumb_773.bmp` that today's Bible tile, the masthead and the sleep screen
    (`APP_STATE.bibleCoverPath`, `LauncherActivity.cpp:108-114`) already use. No new thumbnail
    is generated.
  - The mockup's 470 px is rejected: it would generate a second thumbnail.
  - The hero's height is the remainder after the fixed sections, as the Bible tile absorbs the
    remainder today (`LauncherActivity.cpp:274-277`).
- **A18 — Selection.**
  - Button navigation walks one ring in reading order: Continue, Go to, Recent 1–3 (filled slots
    only), the verse card (when tappable), Meetings, Tags, Search, Publications, Settings.
  - The selected target gets a 3 px border instead of 1 px, never an inversion, following
    `drawTile` (`LauncherActivity.cpp:340-343`).
  - The Continue button is always inverted, as the primary action in the mockup. When it is
    selected, its border is drawn in white inside the fill.
- **A19 — Refresh.** The first paint of an entry is `HALF_REFRESH` iff
  `launcherNeedsCleanPaint(cleanInitialRefresh, firstRenderDone)` (`LauncherRefresh.h:15`), as
  now (`LauncherActivity.cpp:398-404`). The verse fill and every selection move are
  `FAST_REFRESH`. `LauncherRefresh.h` is unchanged.
- **A20 — Rendering layer.** Raw `renderer` and `GUI` calls, as `LauncherActivity` uses today. The
  week strip is re-drawn in that idiom rather than porting Home to FreeInkUI's `UiScreen`, which
  would rewrite every other section of this screen as well.

## Architecture

`LauncherActivity` is rebuilt in place. `ActivityManager::goHome`
(`ActivityManager.cpp:242-244`), `isHomeActivity()` and the `cleanInitialRefresh` constructor
argument are unchanged.

| Unit | Kind | Modelled on |
|---|---|---|
| `src/activities/launcher/HomeLayout.h` | Pure geometry: hero, recent label and slots, verse card, Meetings card, icon row, from `ThemeMetrics`, viewable insets and line heights. Host-tested. | `src/components/MastheadLayout.h`, `test/masthead/` |
| `src/activities/launcher/HomeTargets.h` | Pure routing: what each target does given `hasBible`, the place count and whether a pick exists, returned as an action enum plus a `ReaderEntryIntent::Kind`. Host-tested. | `LauncherRefresh.h` + `test/launcher_refresh/`; `ReaderEntryIntent::route` |
| `src/activities/boot_sleep/StudyPassageScan.{h,cpp}` | `pickPassage`, `offerFile` and `offerRow`, lifted out of the anonymous namespace (`StudySleepScreen.cpp:180-291`) with parameters for the file set (all, or one pubkey), the `FitGate`, the `RandomFn` and the ring (or none). | the code it is lifted from |
| `src/activities/boot_sleep/StudySleepScreen.cpp` | Calls the lifted scan with its current arguments. Sleep behaviour is unchanged. | — |
| `src/activities/boot_sleep/StudySleepPick.h` | `Candidate` gains `study::Unit start`, and a seeded PRNG `RandomFn` is added. Host-tested. | existing `test/study_sleep_pick/` |
| `src/activities/launcher/HomeVerse.{h,cpp}` | The daily RAM cache singleton, plus `ensurePick(renderer, cardTextBox)`. | `CatalogIndexStore::getInstance` |
| `src/activities/launcher/LauncherActivity.{h,cpp}` | Resolve, layout, draw, input and navigation. `LauncherBible.h` resolution is kept, and the meeting-publication lookups (148-235) are kept. | itself |

**New allocations.**
- `HomeVerse`'s `Candidate`: internal SRAM, a few hundred bytes, for the wake (A8).
- The scan's `JsonDocument`: PSRAM through `PsramJsonAllocator::json()`, released when the scan
  returns (`StudySleepScreen.cpp:242`).
- `Sampler` and `ScanBuffers`: through `makeUniqueNoThrow` while the scan runs
  (`StudySleepScreen.cpp:376-381`).
- The Recent rows: `Place out[3]`, a member array. A `Place` holds a `std::string` reference of at
  most 48 bytes (`PlacesDoc.h:25`).
- No new per-frame allocation. Formatted strings go into fixed `char[]` members.

## Data and control flow

1. `onEnter`: `computeLayout()` through `HomeLayout`, then `resolveTargets()`:
   - the Bible, via `resolveBible` as today;
   - `bibleCoverPath` via `CoverBand::thumbPathFor` at the hero's size, mirrored to `APP_STATE`
     as today;
   - the places, via `PLACES.getPlaces()` and `pickRecent`;
   - the meeting cover, week, strip and workbook %;
   - the date line.

   Then the verse: a cache hit for today fills the card now, and a miss sets `versePending`
   (unless there is no Bible, per A15). It logs internal free, largest block and PSRAM free at
   `LOG_INF`, as `BibleNavigationActivity.cpp:78-81` does, and then calls `requestUpdate()`.
2. `render` draws, in order:
   - the hero (`CoverBand::draw` with plate = header + button row, then `GUI.drawHeader` into the
     plate's top, then the buttons);
   - the Recent label and rows;
   - the verse card (pending, filled, or hint);
   - the Meetings card;
   - the icon row.

   It then calls `displayBuffer(mode)` per A19, followed by `PostedMessage::drawNext`.
3. `loop`:
   - If `versePending`: `requestUpdateAndWait()`, then `HomeVerse::ensurePick`, which times the
     scan and logs file bytes, rows, rejects and ms like `StudySleepScreen.cpp:398-402`. Then
     clear the flag, log the heap again, and `requestUpdate()`.
   - Otherwise, a tap hit-tests the targets from `HomeLayout`, and Next, Previous and Confirm walk
     the A18 ring. Both dispatch through `HomeTargets`.
4. Targets call `activityManager.goToReader(biblePath, …, intent)`, `startActivityForResult`
   (Meetings, Publications, Bible download), or `goToSettings()`.

## Error handling

| Failure | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `bible.json` missing, unreadable, over its cap or an unknown format | Logged as in `offerFile` (`StudySleepScreen.cpp:228-251`). The card shows the A11 hint. Nothing is written. |
| OOM for `Sampler`, `ScanBuffers` or the JSON doc | `LOG_ERR`. The card shows the hint for this entry, and the cache stays invalid, so the next entry retries. |
| The place cannot be found in the open edition | The reader shows `STR_LINK_TARGET_NOT_FOUND` (`EpubReaderActivity.cpp:947-951`). No change here. |
| The cover is missing or won't draw | The header is drawn in its place, and the plate buttons sit on paper (A16). |
| The meeting cover, week or workbook is missing | That element is omitted. The card stays tappable into Meetings. |
| The places store is refused (unknown format) | `getPlaces()` is empty (`PlacesStore.cpp:31-39` keeps it empty). Behaves as A2. |

## Testing strategy

TDD. Each pure unit gets its failing test first.

- **`test/home_layout/`** (new, a hand-off line) against the real Lyra and base metric tables,
  with the X4 Pro insets `9/3/3/3` (`BoardConfig.h:625-630`, as `test/masthead` uses). It checks
  that:
  - hero x = 8 and width = 464, and `thumbHeightFor(width, height) == 773`;
  - every section lies inside the viewable area with no overlap;
  - the hero keeps at least its plate plus a minimum art height;
  - four equal icon tiles fill the width.
- **`HomeTargets` tests** (same suite) cover every target × {Bible, no Bible} × {0, 1, 4 places} ×
  {pick, no pick}:
  - Continue gives OpenAt with places and a plain open without them;
  - Go to gives BookGrid, Tags gives Tags, and Search gives Search;
  - with no Bible, Download;
  - empty Recent slots and a pick-less card are not targets.

  This is the host half of "each lands on the right screen".
- **`test/study_sleep_pick/`** is extended:
  - the same date gives the same pick: the seed is derived from `yyyymmdd`, and a fresh `Sampler`
    over the same offers yields the same candidate (d1);
  - a different date gives an independent pick: the seeds differ, and over a fixed set of offers
    the picks for a run of dates are not all the same;
  - the fallback seed is a fixed constant, and the same undated scan picks the same candidate;
  - `Candidate::start` is carried through `Sampler::fill`;
  - the seeded `RandomFn` stays in `[0, bound)`.
- **`test/launcher_refresh/`** is unchanged and must stay green.
- **Build**: `pio run` (x4pro).
- **Device, for the human tester:**
  - Wake to Home. It paints, then the card fills. The log shows the scan ms.
  - Leave Home and return. The log shows a cache hit and no scan.
  - Sleep and wake the same day. The same verse comes back.
  - `LOG_INF` internal free is ≥ ~50 KB after fill.
  - Each target lands on its screen.
  - A wake from the sleep image gives a clean HALF paint.

## Decision record

**d1 (answered by the orchestrator on the owner's behalf; the issue's author).** "Cached once per
day" means the reader sees one verse all day, and Home doesn't re-scan on every entry.

- **Decided:** a date-seeded deterministic pick plus a RAM cache for the wake, with no new stored
  state. The tier stays `standard`.
- **Guardrails, now in the spec:**
  - With the RTC date unknown, use a fixed fallback seed and log it at `LOG_DBG` (A7).
  - Never read or advance the sleep screen's ring (A6).
  - Scan only `bible.json`, within the existing picker bounds, painted after the rest of Home, and
    log the duration once per wake (A4, A6, A10).
  - A tag change mid-day may change the pick (A6, A9).
  - Host-test that the same date gives the same pick and a different date an independent one
    (Testing).
- **Changed since the first draft:** A7 used hardware `random()` with no clock and now uses the
  fixed fallback seed. A6 and A9 gain the tag-change note, and the tests gain the date cases.
