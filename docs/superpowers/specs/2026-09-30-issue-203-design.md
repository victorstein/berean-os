# Issue #203 design: Home redesign for a reference Bible

Status: revision 1, after spec review 0 (`docs/superpowers/reviews/issue-203-spec-review-0.md`).
Research: `docs/superpowers/research/2026-09-30-issue-203-research.md`. Base: `75c65e93`. The
owner's mockup is artifact `4qvfHbNnJ77F2gM5DELQrQ` ("homeProposed").

## Problem

Home (`src/activities/launcher/LauncherActivity.cpp`) is a shelf of four tiles and a resume strip.

- The Bible tile opens the book where it was left (`openBible`, 429-433). Getting back to a
  *place*, the book grid, search or tags means entering the reader and its menu first.
- The resume strip resumes whatever book was opened last (`recents[0]`, 76-80, 421-422).

Issue #201 made places (`PLACES`, `src/PlacesStore.h:42`) and entry intents
(`src/activities/reader/ReaderEntryIntent.h`) available, but no caller produces an intent yet.
Every `goToReader(` call passes the defaults (research, "Places and intents").

## Goal

Home per Direction B v2. Getting back to a place takes one tap, and Home shows no Bible progress.

- **Cover hero** through `CoverBand`, with **Continue · <newest place>** and **Go to** on its
  plate.
- **Recent places**: up to three rows, each opening there (`OpenAt`).
- **From your tags**: a whole tagged Bible passage, picked once per day, painted after the rest of
  Home, and opening its passage (`OpenAt`).
- **Meetings card**: this week's range, the Workbook weekly %, and the week strip
  (`buildWeekStrip`) with the meeting days from Settings.
- **Icon row**: Tags, Search, Publications and Settings.

## Non-goals

- The places store, the intent contract and the reader's consumption of intents stay unchanged.
- `CoverBand`, `Masthead`, and what the sleep screen shows or records stay unchanged.
- No new stored data: nothing new under `/.berean/`, in `state.json`, or in RTC memory (A6, d1).
- No Bible reading progress on Home.
- Tags, Search and Go to open inside the Bible only.
- No new icon art, and no new cover thumbnail size (A13, A17).

## Assumptions (each one is a decision for review to attack)

- **A1 — Continue is the newest place, and the "resume any book" strip goes.**
  - Continue calls `goToReader(biblePath, /*allowFastInitialRefresh=*/true,
    ReaderEntryIntent::openAt(PLACES.getPlaces()[0]))`.
  - The issue defines Continue as "the newest place from the places store". Places are Bible-only
    (`ReaderEntryIntent.h:35`).
  - As a result, a Watchtower or another EPUB is no longer one tap from Home. This week's meeting
    publications are two taps away through the Meetings card, and everything else is reached
    through Publications.
- **A2 — No places yet.**
  - The button reads `tr(STR_CONTINUE_READING)` (`english.yaml:16`) and calls
    `goToReader(biblePath, true)`, opening the Bible at its saved position.
  - Today's Bible tile opens the same book with the default `false` (`LauncherActivity.cpp:431`,
    `ActivityManager.h:86`). The difference is the refresh flag (A19a).
- **A3 — Recent rows.**
  - The rows come from `PlacesDoc::pickRecent(places, places[0].unit, out, 3)`
    (`src/util/PlacesDoc.h:67-69`, `PlacesDoc.cpp:78-87`).
  - Places are unique per (book, chapter) (`PlacesDoc.h:78-79`), so skipping the head's chapter
    skips exactly the head.
  - There are three fixed slots whatever the count, so Home's layout never moves. Empty slots are
    blank and untappable.
  - The label is `tr(STR_RECENT)` (`english.yaml:481`).
- **A4 — The verse card reads the Bible's passages only.**
  - The file is `/.berean/passages/bible.json`: `PASSAGES_DIR` (`lib/Serialization/SdPaths.h:31`)
    plus `study::BIBLE_PUB_KEY` (`lib/StudyStore/StudyStore/PubKey.h`).
  - OpenAt resolves only in the Bible (`ReaderEntryIntent.h:36-39`), and `locatePlace` refuses any
    other pubkey (review 0, `StudyStore.cpp:102-104`).
  - One file is also the tightest bound, `MAX_FILE_BYTES = SAVE_BYTE_BUDGET + 4096`
    (`StudySleepScreen.cpp:42`, `PassageDoc.h:38`). The owner confirmed this scope in d1.
- **A5 — The pick uses the sleep picker's rules with a card-sized fit gate.**
  - Rows are filtered exactly as `offerRow` does: addressable start, whole text, within the
    prefilter, and fits (`StudySleepScreen.cpp:189-223`).
  - Sampling is `study_sleep::Sampler` (`StudySleepPick.h:103-148`).
  - Fit is decided before sampling (`StudySleepPick.h:98-102`) through `fitPassage`
    (`StudySleepFit.h:101-121`), against the card's text box (A21). The rungs, in order:

    | Rung | Font | Line height | Lines in the 102 px box |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | 1 | `NOTOSERIF_14_FONT_ID` italic | 40 | 2 |
    | 2 | `NOTOSERIF_12_FONT_ID` italic | 34 | 3 |
    | 3 | `UI_10_FONT_ID` regular | 24 | 4 |

    Line heights are the regular faces' `advanceY`, which is what `getLineHeight` returns
    (`GfxRenderer.cpp:2141-2149`; `notoserif_14_regular.h:4104`, `notoserif_12_regular.h:3704`,
    `ubuntu_10_regular.h:3719`). The font IDs and styles are the sleep screen's
    (`StudySleepScreen.cpp:61-64`).
  - A passage that doesn't fit whole is never shown cut, as on the sleep screen (#188).
  - Consequence: only passages of roughly 3–4 card lines are eligible. That is accepted: the card
    is a glance, and the reader, one tap away, shows the whole passage.
- **A6 — "Once per day" means one verse all day, reached by seeding the pick with the date; the
  cache lasts for the wake.** (Decided, d1.)
  - **What survives sleep.** Deep sleep resets the heap and `.bss`: `enterDeepSleep` reaches a plain
    `esp_deep_sleep_start()` (`freeink-sdk/libs/hardware/PowerManager/src/PowerManager.cpp:96`).
    RTC slow memory does survive. The repo already keeps data there with `RTC_NOINIT_ATTR`: the log
    ring (`lib/Logging/Logging.cpp:12-19`) and the silent-restart flags (`src/main.cpp:137-139`).
    Revision 0 wrongly said nothing in RAM survives (review 0, MAJOR 1).
  - **Why RTC memory is still declined.** Every existing RTC use is diagnostic or restart state,
    guarded by a magic word. A pick cached there would be a new persisted format outside the store
    rules in CLAUDE.md: it needs a magic word, a version, cold-boot garbage handling, and a check
    that the 8 KB region has room, which review 0 measured at about 2.6 KB free. d1 settled "no new
    stored state". The seed already makes the verse the same across wakes, so RTC memory would only
    save the scan, and that scan is bounded (A4) and painted after Home (A10).
  - **The seed.** The sampler's `RandomFn` (`StudySleepPick.h:88`) is a seeded generator: the seed
    is `fnv1a32(FNV_OFFSET_BASIS, "yyyymmdd")` over the local date's eight digits
    (`StudySleepPick.h:31-40`), and each draw is one splitmix32 step reduced to `[0, bound)`.
    Hashing the date first means consecutive days start from unrelated states (review 0, MINOR 2).
    One file, read in file order, with the same seed gives the same pick all day while the
    passages are unchanged.
  - **The RAM cache.** The pick is kept in RAM for the wake (A8), so later Home entries in that
    wake don't scan. The scan runs once per wake, on that wake's first Home entry, and its
    duration is logged then.
  - **This is how the acceptance criterion is met.** "Later entries don't re-scan" holds within a
    wake. After a wake, one scan runs and paints the same verse. This is d1's reading of the issue.
  - **The sleep ring.** The recent-sleep ring (`CrossPointState.h:31-33`) is never read or written,
    and `pushRecentStudySleep` (`CrossPointState.h:53`) is never called from Home, so the two
    pickers stay independent (d1).
  - **Tag changes.** Adding or removing tags can change the pick, because the sampler sees
    different rows. That is accepted (d1).
- **A7 — No clock.**
  - When `readLocalDate` fails (`src/util/LocalDate.h:8`), the seed is the fixed constant
    `HOME_VERSE_FALLBACK_SEED`, logged at `LOG_DBG` (d1). It is never hardware random.
  - The cache key's date is marked "undated", so the undated pick is stable across wakes and never
    thrashes.
  - A clock that has a date but no local time (`shifted == false`) still gives a date, and that
    date seeds the pick. The day may then turn over at UTC midnight rather than local midnight,
    which is harmless for the verse.
- **A8 — The cache is a RAM singleton.**
  - It is modelled on `CatalogIndexStore::getInstance()` (`src/network/CatalogIndexStore.cpp:36-39`),
    a non-persistent singleton whose state (the stamp) outlives the screen that loaded it.
  - It holds: `{bool valid; bool dated; CivilDate day; uint32_t fileBytes; bool hasPick;
    HomeVerseEmpty emptyReason; study_sleep::Candidate pick;}`.
  - **Key.** The cache is valid for a Home entry when its `dated`/`day` match today and
    `fileBytes` matches `bible.json`'s current size. The size comes from one `Storage` open with no
    parse (review 0, MINOR 3), so most tag edits re-key it (A9).
  - **Empty results are cached.** A completed scan with no pick is cached (`hasPick = false`), so a
    user whose passages all fail to fit does not re-parse on every entry (review 0, MINOR 3). An
    OOM is **not** cached.
  - **Memory.** `pick.text` is a `std::string` bounded by what fits the card: four lines of Ubuntu
    10, a few hundred bytes. It sits in internal SRAM, under the 4096-byte PSRAM auto-routing
    threshold. That is accepted: it is small, lives for the wake, and is replaced at most when the
    key changes, so it does not fragment.
- **A9 — Staleness.**
  - Keying on `bible.json`'s size means a tag added or removed in the reader re-scans on the next
    Home entry. The pick may change then, which d1 accepts.
  - An edit that leaves the size exactly the same keeps the cached pick for the wake. Its tap still
    opens its verse, because the address is the unit, not the row.
- **A10 — Paint first, then fill.** This mirrors `MeetingsActivity::loop`
  (`src/activities/network/MeetingsActivity.cpp:285-293`).
  - On a cache miss, `onEnter` sets `versePending`, and the first `render` draws the card with its
    label and an empty body.
  - `loop()` sees the flag and calls `requestUpdateAndWait()`, so Home is on the panel. It then
    scans, fills the cache and calls `requestUpdate()`.
  - The scan runs on the loop task, not in a new FreeRTOS task. A task would share the renderer's
    font measurement (`measurePassage`, `StudySleepScreen.cpp:133-136`) with the render task, which
    nothing here does today.
  - The cost is that input is not polled during the scan, so a tap that starts and ends inside it
    can be missed. The scan is bounded (A4) and timed in the log, and the device check judges it.
- **A11 — The card with no pick.** The card keeps its slot and label, is not tappable, and shows
  one line whose wording depends on why (review 0, MINOR 3):
  - no `bible.json`, or no whole row: `STR_HOME_TAGS_EMPTY`, "Passages you tag appear here";
  - whole rows exist but none fits the card: `STR_HOME_TAGS_TOO_LONG`, "Your tagged passages are
    too long to show here". The scan already counts `rowsUnfit` and `rowsOverPrefilter`
    (`StudySleepScreen.cpp:95-102`).
- **A12 — Tapping the card** calls `goToReader(biblePath, false, intent{kind = OpenAt, unit =
  pick.start, spineHint = pick.spine})`. (Revision 0 said `spineHint = 0`; see review 0, MAJOR 2.)
  - `Candidate` gains `study::Unit start`, parsed from the row's `"u"`. `offerRow` already
    validates it with `study::unitFromCompact` (`StudySleepScreen.cpp:191-192`).
  - `Candidate` also gains `uint16_t spine`, read from the row's `"s"`. `PassageDoc::toJson` writes
    the passage's `documentSpine` there (`lib/StudyStore/StudyStore/PassageDoc.cpp:279`), and
    `fromJson` reads it as `v["s"] | 0` (`:321`).
  - The reader's own passage lookup passes that spine as the hint:
    `locateUnit(passage.start, passage.documentSpine)`, in `StudyStore::locate(passageIndex)`
    (`src/study/StudyStore.cpp:156-159`).
  - This matters because only a Verse unit can be searched for without a correct hint
    (`StudyStore.cpp:191-194`, "Only a Verse address is portable enough to search for"). A passage
    tagged in study notes or an appendix opens only through its spine.
  - `StudySleepPick.h` stays Arduino-free. `StudyStore/Unit.h` is already included by the
    host-tested `ReaderEntryIntent.h`.
- **A13 — The Meetings card.**
  - A tap pushes `MeetingsActivity`, as the Meetings tile does today
    (`LauncherActivity.cpp:448-451`).
  - **No cover art.** Meetings sizes its covers to half-screen cards (`MeetingsActivity.cpp:111-120`),
    so no cached thumbnail fits a compact card. A card-sized one would be a new thumbnail size,
    generated by opening the EPUB before the first paint (review 0, MINOR 6). The card draws
    `LibraryIcon` (`src/components/icons/library.h`, today's Meetings tile icon at
    `LauncherActivity.cpp:385`) in the cover's place. This departs from the mockup's small cover.
  - **The week** is the current ISO week, from `readLocalDate` and `isoWeekFromUtcDate`, as
    `thisWeeksMeetingPublication` does (`LauncherActivity.cpp:149-154`). Its Monday comes from
    `mondayOfIsoWeek`, as `MeetingsActivity.cpp:197-201` does.
  - **The range line** is `formatWeekRange(monday, tr(STR_MEETING_WEEK_RANGE),
    tr(STR_MEETING_WEEK_RANGE_SPAN), tr(STR_MONTHS_LONG), …)` (`MeetingWeekView.h:31-32`,
    `MeetingsActivity.cpp:202-203`).
  - **The strip** is `buildWeekStrip(monday, todayIsLocal ? &today : nullptr,
    SETTINGS.midweekMeetingDay, SETTINGS.weekendMeetingDay)`. Meetings passes `nullptr` too when
    the time is unknown, because "a UTC date would mark the wrong day" (`MeetingsActivity.cpp:156,
    208-210`; review 0, MINOR 1). The initials come from `copyInitial` over `WEEKDAY_NAME_IDS`
    (`src/util/WeekdayNames.h:10`, `MeetingsActivity.cpp:213-215`).
  - **Two cases without a clock.** With no date at all, the range and strip are omitted. With a
    date but no local time, both are drawn, and no day is marked.
  - **The %** is `readBookProgressPercent(workbookPath)`, formatted with `STR_MEETING_PROGRESS`
    ("%d%% read") (`MeetingsActivity.cpp:274-278`).
    - The workbook path is `MeetingLibrary::findPublication(MeetingPub::Workbook,
      entry->workbook)`, from the `MeetingWeekCache` entry for this week (`LauncherActivity.cpp:156-163`).
    - With no entry, no workbook, or no progress, the line is omitted.
  - **The card title** is `tr(STR_MEETINGS)` (`LauncherActivity.cpp:384`).
- **A14 — The icon row.**
  - **Tags**: `goToReader(biblePath, false, ReaderEntryIntent::of(Kind::Tags))`, with
    `BookmarkIcon` (`src/components/icons/bookmark.h`), since there is no tag icon.
  - **Search**: `of(Kind::Search)`, with `SearchIcon`.
  - **Publications**: pushes `PublicationsActivity` and re-resolves on return
    (`LauncherActivity.cpp:453-459`), with `LibraryIcon`.
  - **Settings**: `activityManager.goToSettings()` (`LauncherActivity.cpp:461`), with
    `Settings2Icon`.
  - Labels: `STR_TAGS` (`english.yaml:375`), `STR_SEARCH` (288), `STR_PUBLICATIONS` (434) and
    `STR_SETTINGS_TITLE` (14).
  - **Go to** on the plate: `of(Kind::BookGrid)`, labelled `tr(STR_GO_TO)` ("Go to"; "Ir a" in
    `spanish.yaml:423`). The existing key is reused without the mockup's ellipsis (review 0, MINOR 7).
- **A15 — No Bible on the card** (`biblePath` empty after `resolveBible`,
  `LauncherActivity.cpp:90-103`).
  - The plate carries its title and a single `tr(STR_DOWNLOAD)` button (`english.yaml:223`) that
    runs today's download path (`openBible`, 434-445).
  - Tags and Search route there too.
  - The Recent rows and the verse card render empty, and nothing is scanned.
- **A16 — Header and date.**
  - There is no separate header. The plate carries the theme header,
    `GUI.drawHeader(renderer, plateHeaderRect, tr(STR_BIBLE), dateLine)`, as `Masthead::draw`
    does (`src/components/Masthead.cpp`, `draw`), so the battery and the #185 charging state stay
    in the theme.
  - `dateLine` comes from `formatDateLine` (`StudySleepPick.h:179-199`), which needs both date
    and time. `drawHeader` right-aligns it beside the battery (`BaseTheme.cpp:321-335`). With no
    clock it is null.
  - When the cover can't be drawn, the header goes in its usual place, as `Masthead`'s fallback
    does, and no text is ever drawn over dither.
- **A17 — The hero shares the Masthead band's left edge and width.**
  - The band is `MastheadLayout::band(screenW, marginTop, marginRight, marginLeft, topPadding,
    heroHeight)` (`src/components/MastheadLayout.h:23-28`): x = 8 and width = 464 under Lyra with
    insets 9/3/3/3.
  - `thumbHeightFor(464, h) == 773` for h ≤ 773 (`CoverBandGeometry.h:46-48`), so the hero uses
    the `thumb_773.bmp` that the Bible tile, the masthead and the sleep screen already share
    (`LauncherActivity.cpp:108-114`).
  - The mockup's 470 px is rejected because it would generate a second thumbnail.
  - The hero's height is the remainder after the fixed sections (A21), as the Bible tile absorbs
    the remainder today (`LauncherActivity.cpp:274-277`).
- **A18 — Selection.**
  - Button navigation walks one ring in reading order: Continue (or Download), Go to, the filled
    Recent slots, the verse card when tappable, Meetings, Tags, Search, Publications, Settings.
  - The selected target gets a 3 px border instead of 1 px, never an inversion, as `drawTile`
    does (`LauncherActivity.cpp:340-343`).
  - Continue is always inverted, as the primary action. When selected, its border is drawn in
    white inside the fill.
- **A19 — Panel refresh.**
  - The first paint of an entry is `HALF_REFRESH` iff `launcherNeedsCleanPaint(cleanInitialRefresh,
    firstRenderDone)` (`LauncherRefresh.h:15`; `LauncherActivity.cpp:398-404`).
  - The verse fill and every selection move are `FAST_REFRESH`.
  - `LauncherRefresh.h` is unchanged.
- **A19a — The reader's first-page refresh budget** (`allowFastInitialRefresh`,
  `ReaderActivity.cpp:19-22`). This is chosen per target (review 0, MINOR 5):
  - **Continue** passes `true`, with or without places. It replaces the old resume strip, the
    wake-to-carry-on path, which passed `true` (`LauncherActivity.cpp:422`).
  - **Every other reader target** passes `false`, like today's Bible tile
    (`LauncherActivity.cpp:431`): Recent, the verse card, Go to, Tags and Search.
- **A20 — Rendering layer.** Raw `renderer` and `GUI` calls, as `LauncherActivity` uses today. The
  week strip is re-drawn in that idiom from `WeekStripCell`, rather than porting Home to
  FreeInkUI's `UiScreen` (`MeetingsActivity.cpp:318-350`).
- **A21 — Section heights** (review 0, MINOR 4). All come from `HomeLayout` and are pinned in
  `test/home_layout`.
  - **Inputs:**
    - line heights passed in: `small` = 23 (`notosans_8_regular.h:3663`), `ui10` = 24, `serif12`
      = 34, `serif14` = 40;
    - `ThemeMetrics`: `topPadding`, `headerHeight`, `verticalSpacing` and `listRowHeight`;
    - file-local `PAD = 8` and `ICON = 32`, as today (`LauncherActivity.cpp:50-53`).
  - **Gaps:** there are four gaps of `verticalSpacing` between the five sections.

  | Section | Formula | Lyra | Base |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Icon row | `ICON + small + 3·PAD` | 79 | 79 |
  | Meetings card | `ui10 + 2·small + 2·PAD` (title, range, %); the strip, `small + ui10 + PAD`, sits beside it and fits inside | 86 | 86 |

  - The text box is 102 px at every rung. Rung by rung that is 2, 3 and 4 lines (A5).
  - The card carries no tag pill, which departs from the mockup. A pill would mean reading the tag

## Architecture

`LauncherActivity` is rebuilt in place. `ActivityManager::goHome` (`ActivityManager.cpp:242-244`),
`isHomeActivity()` and the `cleanInitialRefresh` constructor argument are unchanged.

| Unit | Role | Modelled on |
|---|---|---|
| `src/activities/launcher/HomeLayout.h` | Pure geometry for A17 and A21, including the text-box rungs. Host-tested. | `src/components/MastheadLayout.h`, `test/masthead/` |
| `src/activities/launcher/HomeTargets.h` | Pure routing: target × (`hasBible`, place count, `hasPick`) → action and `ReaderEntryIntent::Kind`, plus the A19a refresh flag. Host-tested. | `LauncherRefresh.h`, `test/launcher_refresh/`; `ReaderEntryIntent::route` |
| `src/activities/boot_sleep/StudyPassageScan.{h,cpp}` | `pickPassage`, `offerFile` and `offerRow` lifted out of the anonymous namespace (`StudySleepScreen.cpp:180-291`), parameterised by the file set (all, or one pubkey), `FitGate`, `RandomFn`, and ring or none. `offerRow` now also fills `start` and `spine`. | the code it is lifted from |
| `src/activities/boot_sleep/StudySleepScreen.cpp` | Calls the lifted scan with its current arguments. Sleep behaviour is unchanged. | — |
| `src/activities/boot_sleep/StudySleepPick.h` | `Candidate` gains `start` and `spine`. Adds `DailySeed` (FNV of `yyyymmdd`, the fallback constant) and a splitmix32 `RandomFn`. Host-tested. | existing `test/study_sleep_pick/` |
| `src/activities/launcher/HomeVerse.{h,cpp}` | The A8 cache singleton, and `ensurePick(renderer, textBox)`. | `CatalogIndexStore::getInstance` |
| `src/activities/launcher/LauncherActivity.{h,cpp}` | Resolve, layout, draw, input. Keeps `LauncherBible.h` resolution and the meeting lookups (`LauncherActivity.cpp:148-235`). | itself |

**New allocations.**
- `HomeVerse`'s pick: internal SRAM, a few hundred bytes, for the wake (A8).
- The scan's `JsonDocument`: PSRAM through `PsramJsonAllocator::json()`, released when the scan
  returns (`StudySleepScreen.cpp:242`).
- `Sampler` and `ScanBuffers`: `makeUniqueNoThrow` for the scan's duration
  (`StudySleepScreen.cpp:376-381`).
- The Recent places: a member `Place out[3]`, each holding a reference string of at most 48 bytes
  (`PlacesDoc.h:25`).
- Formatted lines go into fixed `char[]` members. Nothing is allocated per frame.

## Data and control flow

1. `onEnter`:
   - `computeLayout()` through `HomeLayout`.
   - `resolveTargets()`:
     - the Bible (`resolveBible`, as today);
     - `bibleCoverPath` at the hero size, mirrored to `APP_STATE` as today;
     - the places and `pickRecent`;
     - the week, range, strip and workbook % (A13);
     - the date line (A16).
   - The verse: if there is a Bible and `HomeVerse` is valid for today's key (A8), the card is
     filled now. Otherwise `versePending` is set.
   - Log internal free, largest block and PSRAM free at `LOG_INF`, as
     `BibleNavigationActivity.cpp:78-81` does.
   - `requestUpdate()`.
2. `render`:
   - the hero (`CoverBand::draw` with the A21 plate, `GUI.drawHeader` into the plate top, then the
     buttons);
   - Recent;
   - the verse card (pending, filled, or A11 hint);
   - Meetings;
   - the icon row;
   - `displayBuffer(mode)` per A19, then `PostedMessage::drawNext`.
3. `loop`:
   - If `versePending`: `requestUpdateAndWait()`, then `HomeVerse::ensurePick`. That seeds per
     A6/A7, scans `bible.json`, and logs bytes, rows, rejects and ms like
     `StudySleepScreen.cpp:398-402`. Then clear the flag, log the heap again, and `requestUpdate()`.
   - Otherwise, a tap hit-tests the `HomeLayout` targets, and Next, Previous and Confirm walk the
     A18 ring. Both dispatch through `HomeTargets`.
4. Targets call `activityManager.goToReader(biblePath, flag, intent)`, `startActivityForResult`
   (Meetings, Publications, the Bible download), or `goToSettings()`.

## Error handling

| Failure | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `bible.json` missing, unreadable, over its cap, or an unknown format | Logged as in `offerFile` (`StudySleepScreen.cpp:228-251`). The result is cached as no pick, and the card shows the A11 hint. Nothing is written. |
| OOM for `Sampler`, `ScanBuffers` or the JSON document | `LOG_ERR`. The card shows `STR_HOME_TAGS_EMPTY` for this entry. The result is not cached, so the next entry retries. |
| The place or passage cannot be found in the open edition | The reader shows `STR_LINK_TARGET_NOT_FOUND` (`EpubReaderActivity.cpp:947-951`). Unchanged. |
| The cover is missing or won't draw | The header is drawn in its usual place, and the plate buttons sit on paper (A16). |
| No week entry, workbook or progress | That line is omitted. The card stays tappable into Meetings. |
| The places store is refused | `getPlaces()` is empty (`PlacesStore.cpp:31-51`). Behaves as A2. |

## Testing strategy

TDD. Each pure unit gets its failing test first.

- **`test/home_layout/`** (new; a hand-off line) against the real Lyra and base metric tables, with
  insets 9/3/3/3 (`BoardConfig.h:624-629`, as `test/masthead` does) and the A21 line heights. It
  checks that:
  - the hero is at x = 8 with width 464, and `thumbHeightFor == 773`;
  - every section's height matches the A21 table;
  - the sections stay inside the viewable area without overlapping;
  - the hero's art is at least `MIN_HERO_ART`;

## Decision record

**d1 (answered by the orchestrator for the issue's author).** "Cached once per day" means one
verse all day, with no re-scan on every Home entry. The decision is a date-seeded deterministic
pick plus a RAM cache for the wake, with no new stored state, so the tier stays `standard`. Its
guardrails:

- a fixed fallback seed at `LOG_DBG` when there is no date (A7);
- never touch the sleep ring (A6);
- scan only `bible.json` within the picker's bounds, painted after Home, and log the duration once
  per wake (A4, A6, A10);
- a tag change mid-day may change the pick (A6, A9);
- host-test "same date, same pick" and "different date, independent pick".
