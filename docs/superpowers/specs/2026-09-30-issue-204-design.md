# Issue #204 design: cover mastheads on the chapter grid, Meetings and Publications

Research: `docs/superpowers/research/2026-09-30-issue-204-research.md`. Base `0275a5bd`.
Modelled on: `LauncherActivity::drawCoverTile` (`src/activities/launcher/LauncherActivity.cpp:302-322`)
for using `CoverBand`; `src/components/CoverBandGeometry.h` + `test/cover_band/` for a renderer-free,
host-tested layout header; `BibleSearchActivity`'s `fullRefreshPending`
(`BibleSearchActivity.h:116`, `.cpp:1019,1035`) for choosing HALF vs FAST per paint;
`BibleNavigationActivity.cpp:69-72` for the heap log line.

## Problem

The Bible chapter grid, Meetings and Publications each draw the compact text header
(`BibleNavigationActivity.cpp:653-672`; `UiListActivity.cpp:142-147` for the other two). Direction B
v2 wants screens that have a cover to show it: a band of cover art with the title on an opaque plate.
`CoverBand` (#202) can draw the art (`src/components/CoverBand.cpp:64-84`), but no screen outside the
launcher uses it, and nothing lays out a masthead or decides how a screen with one refreshes.

## Goal

1. A shared **masthead**: a cover band about 120 px tall, drawn through `CoverBand`, with the
   title and battery on an opaque plate along its lower edge. It replaces the compact header and
   the title's row on the three screens.
2. The chapter grid keeps its 7 × 10 square grid and markers from #199.
3. Selection moves stay on FAST refresh. Entry from the launcher takes one HALF.
4. Heap is logged on entry to each of the three screens.

## Non-goals

- Settings, Tags and every other screen keep the compact header (issue #204, "Screens without a
  cover").
- The book level of the Bible grid keeps the compact header (A3).
- No PSRAM band cache in this change (A9); the masthead logs its draw time so the device test can
  decide.
- No "‹ Books" affordance on the plate as in the mockup: Back is the left-edge swipe
  (`MappedInputManager.cpp`, per `CLAUDE.md` "Input hardware"), and the input layer is out of scope.
- No change to `CoverBand`, `CoverThumb`, the launcher, the sleep screen, or any on-disk format.
- No new translation keys: the titles already exist (`STR_MEETINGS`, `STR_PUBLICATIONS`, the grid's
  book name and `STR_SELECT_*`, `BibleNavigationActivity.cpp:656-666`).

## Assumptions (each one a decision for the review to attack)

- **A1 — Band geometry mirrors the launcher's Bible tile.** The band is inset by `topPadding` on the
  left, right and top, inside the viewable insets, exactly as `LauncherActivity::computeLayout` derives
  `left`/`right`/`width` (`LauncherActivity.cpp:258-262`). On the X4 Pro that is x = 5, y = 5,
  470 × 120. Why: `thumbHeightFor(470, 120) = max(120, int(470 / 0.6)) = 783`
  (`CoverBandGeometry.h:46-48`), the same `thumb_783.bmp` the launcher's Bible tile already generates
  and caches (`LauncherActivity.cpp:107-108`). A full-bleed 480 band would need a second
  `thumb_800.bmp` per publication, and generating one opens the EPUB. The mockup's full-bleed art is
  hand-drawn, not a device render.
- **A2 — Height is a theme metric, `mastheadHeight = 120`,** added to `ThemeMetrics`
  (`src/components/themes/BaseTheme.h:25`) with a value in both `BaseMetrics::values`
  (`BaseTheme.h:127`) and `LyraMetrics::values` (`LyraTheme.h:9`), because `CLAUDE.md` routes every
  position through `UITheme` and `headerHeight`/`tabBarHeight` live there already. 120 is the issue's
  number. It does not cost the grid a row: body = 800 − (5 + 120) − 8 = 667 → `(667 + 8) / 64 = 10`
  rows, square cell `min((480 − 48) / 7, (667 − 72) / 10) = min(61, 59) = 59` ≥ `MIN_CELL 56`
  (`NumberGridLayout.h:12-20,33-47`). The largest height keeping 10 rows is 155 (body 632 = 800 − 5 − 155 − 8). A host
  test pins this (T4), so the band never needs to shrink at 120.
- **A3 — On the grid, the masthead is shown at the Chapter and Verse levels only.** The issue names
  the chapter grid. The verse grid is the same `NumberGrid` path and title (`:657-666`), so it gets
  the same chrome. The Book level already spends 40 px on its section sub-header
  (`subHeaderHeight()`, `:271-273`) and lays out 66 books with its own `BookGrid`. Its coverage is
  checked only at runtime (`LOG_ERR` at `:639-641`), so taking another 76 px there risks books
  falling off the page with nothing but a log line.
- **A4 — The plate is the existing theme header, drawn into the plate rect.** `GUI.drawHeader`
  (`BaseTheme.cpp:285-397`) fills its rect first through `fui::header` (`header.h:63-66`) with the
  popup style, which defaults to solid white (`FreeInkUI.cpp:63-73,101`; bereanOS does not override
  `popup` in `UIThemeTokens.h`). It then draws the title, the bottom underline (`:362-366`) and the
  battery (`:369-384`). So the plate is opaque, carries the battery, and looks like every other title
  without new drawing code. `CoverBand`'s `plateHeight` is `headerHeight + 1`. The extra top row
  keeps `CoverBand`'s 1 px rule (`CoverBand.cpp:81`), and the header is drawn in the `headerHeight`
  rows beneath it, so its fill does not erase the rule.
- **A5 — Square corners, no border.** `cornerRadius = 0` (`CoverBand.h:17-18`), because the header's
  square fill would cover rounded bottom corners anyway. A masthead is not selectable, so it has no
  selection border (unlike `drawCoverTile`'s `:321`).
- **A6 — The band is reserved whenever the screen (or grid level) has a masthead, whether or not
  a cover is found.** With no cover, or on a failed draw, the compact header is drawn at its usual
  place (`Rect{0, topPadding, screenWidth, headerHeight}`, as `UiListActivity.cpp:146`) and the rest
  of the band stays paper. Why: Meetings already fixes its layout for the whole visit because the card
  thumbnail size follows it (`MeetingsActivity.h:66-68`). A download on the screen can bring a cover
  that was absent, and letting the content jump or the card thumbnails change size mid-visit would
  break that rule.
- **A7 — Cover per screen:**
  - *Grid:* the open Bible's own `thumb_<thumbHeightFor(band)>.bmp`, i.e.
    `epub->getThumbBmpPath(h)` (`lib/Epub/Epub.h:62`), used **only if it already exists**
    (`Storage.exists`). It is never generated from the reader overlay, which runs with the reader's
    glyph arenas and a loaded EPUB (`BibleNavigationActivity.cpp:57-63`). The launcher generates this
    exact file on every visit home (A1), so in practice it exists. Focus
    `CoverBandGeometry::BOOK_TITLE_BAND`.
  - *Meetings:* the first card with a publication on the card, in `buildCards` order (Watchtower,
    then workbook, `MeetingsActivity.cpp:195`). This matches the launcher's "this week's
    Watchtower, else the workbook" (`LauncherActivity.cpp:117-135`). Focus
    `MAGAZINE_MASTHEAD_BAND`, which is what the launcher's Meetings tile uses (`:386`).
  - *Publications:* the listed publication that was opened most recently, i.e. the first
    `RECENT_BOOKS` entry whose path is in `entries_` (recents are newest first, `recents[0]` is
    "resume", `LauncherActivity.cpp:76-79`). If none has been opened, the first listed entry. With an
    empty card there is no cover (A6). Focus `BOOK_TITLE_BAND`. The issue says "their own cover art"
    and a library has no single cover; this is the least surprising pick.
- **A8 — Refresh.** `UiListActivity` gains a protected `std::atomic<bool> halfRefreshPending{false}`,
  consumed once per paint in `render()` (`displayBuffer(pending ? HALF_REFRESH : FAST_REFRESH)`,
  replacing the bare `displayBuffer()` at `UiListActivity.cpp:169`). This lifts
  `BibleSearchActivity`'s field (`.h:116`, `.cpp:1019,1035`) into the base with a default of false,
  so every other list screen keeps its FAST paint unchanged. It is set:
  - in `MeetingsActivity::onEnter` and `PublicationsActivity::onEnter`: both are only entered from the
    launcher (`LauncherActivity.cpp:449,454`), the art-heavy screen the issue allows one HALF from;
  - in `BibleNavigationActivity::enterLevel` when leaving Chapter/Verse for Book, the one paint
    that replaces art with paper (Back from the chapter grid, `:440-441`). A FAST refresh is
    differential, so the masthead could ghost under the book grid.

  Everything else is FAST, including the grid's entry. It is entered from the reader
  (`EpubReaderActivity.cpp:723`), a text screen, and the band only *adds* ink there. Book → Chapter
  also stays FAST for the same reason. Returning to Meetings or Publications from a child (reader,
  download, search) does not re-run `onEnter`, so those paints stay FAST.
- **A9 — Stream every draw; no PSRAM cache yet.** The issue makes the cache conditional ("If that is
  visibly slow on the device"). Each draw streams the 783-row thumbnail through two transient row
  buffers under 4096 B each, so internal SRAM, freed on return (`CoverBand.cpp:32-37`). No new
  resident allocation. `Masthead::draw` logs the blit's duration (`LOG_DBG`, `millis()` delta, as
  `EpubReaderActivity.cpp:382`). If the device shows a visibly slow selection move, a follow-up adds
  the 7,200 B cache, allocated with `heap_caps_malloc(MALLOC_CAP_SPIRAM | MALLOC_CAP_8BIT)` behind
  a `PsramFree` deleter (`CatalogIndexStore.h:83-90`, `.cpp:40-47`). It is never allocated in
  internal SRAM.
- **A10 — Heap log.** Meetings and Publications log on entry, after their SD work, in the grid's format
  (`BibleNavigationActivity.cpp:69-72`: internal free, largest internal block, PSRAM free). The grid's
  existing line satisfies the criterion there.
- **A11 — Content below the masthead starts at the band's bottom**, `marginTop + topPadding +
  mastheadHeight` (125 on the X4 Pro), then each screen's existing `verticalSpacing` gap. On Meetings
  that moves `top` (`MeetingsActivity.cpp:64`) down by 76 px. The two cards split the loss (38 px
  each, `:94-97`), so `coverHeight` (`:100-101`) shrinks and the card thumbnails are generated
  once at the new height. `fillCard` already falls back to the placeholder when a cover does not fit
  (`:238-245`).

## Architecture

### New: `src/components/MastheadLayout.h` (renderer-free, host-tested)

Modelled on `CoverBandGeometry.h`: `constexpr`, `<algorithm>` only, plain ints.

```cpp
namespace MastheadLayout {
struct Box { int x = 0; int y = 0; int width = 0; int height = 0; };
// Mirrors LauncherActivity::computeLayout's left/right (A1), so the band's
// thumbnail height matches the launcher's Bible tile.
constexpr Box band(int screenWidth, int marginTop, int marginRight, int marginLeft, int topPadding, int height);
constexpr int contentTop(const Box& band);            // band.y + band.height
constexpr Box plate(const Box& band, int headerHeight);   // bottom headerHeight + 1 rows (A4)
constexpr Box plateHeader(const Box& plate);              // plate less its top rule row
}
```

### New: `src/components/Masthead.{h,cpp}`

```cpp
namespace Masthead {
Rect bandRect(const GfxRenderer& renderer);        // MastheadLayout::band from getOrientedViewableTRBL + metrics
int contentTop(const GfxRenderer& renderer);
int thumbHeight(const GfxRenderer& renderer);      // CoverBandGeometry::thumbHeightFor(band)
std::string thumbPathFor(const GfxRenderer& renderer, const std::string& bookPath, bool& generatedAny);
// Cover band + header plate; false (compact header drawn instead, A6) when the
// cover is absent or CoverBand::draw declines or fails.
bool draw(const GfxRenderer& renderer, const std::string& coverPath, float focusBand, const char* title);
}
```

`draw`: `CoverBand::draw(renderer, coverPath, band, Style{focusBand, 0, headerHeight + 1})`, timing it
(A9). On `true` it calls `GUI.drawHeader(renderer, plateHeader, title)`. On `false` it calls
`GUI.drawHeader(renderer, Rect{0, topPadding, screenWidth, headerHeight}, title)`. `CoverBand`
has already cleared the band on a mid-stream failure (`CoverBand.cpp:40-43`), so nothing is drawn
over dither. It holds no state and allocates nothing beyond what `CoverBand` does.

## Data and control flow

**Grid.** Reader menu → `BibleNavigationActivity::onEnter` → `mastheadCover =
Storage.exists(epub->getThumbBmpPath(Masthead::thumbHeight(renderer))) ? path : ""`. Each render:
`drawChrome` → `Masthead::draw` (stream 783 rows, blit 470 × 120, header plate) → `renderUi` →
`buildScreen` with the body under the band → `keyGrid` + markers → FAST. Back to Book → HALF once,
compact header.

**Meetings.** Launcher → `onEnter`: `halfRefreshPending = true`, then `computeLayout` (masthead top)
→ `refresh` (cards + masthead thumb `thumb_783` of the first available card, generated if missing)
→ heap log. First render HALF, later ones FAST. A return from a download re-runs `refresh()`, which
may now find a cover. The band then fills in on a FAST paint.

**Publications.** Launcher → `onEnter`: `halfRefreshPending = true`, `refresh` (entries, row thumbs,
masthead pick + `thumb_783`) → heap log → HALF, then FAST.

## Error handling

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| No cover (none on card, no thumbnail, generation fails, grid's thumbnail absent) | Empty path → `CoverBand` returns false (`CoverBand.cpp:21`) → compact header, band paper (A6). |
| Cover narrower than 0.6 or smaller than the band | `crop.fits == false` (`CoverBandGeometry.h:36`) → same fallback. |
| Row-buffer OOM | `CoverBand` logs `OOM: cover row buffers` (`:34-37`) and returns false → fallback. |
| Read fails mid-stream | `CoverBand` clears the band and returns false (`:40-43`) → fallback header on paper. |

Each is logged by `CoverBand` or `CoverThumb`. Nothing is written, so there is no partial-file case.

## Testing strategy

**Host (TDD, new suite `test/masthead/`, modelled on `test/cover_band/CMakeLists.txt`):**
`MastheadLayoutTest.cpp` against `MastheadLayout.h`, with `CoverBandGeometry.h` and
`activities/reader/NumberGridLayout.h`, all renderer-free under `${REPO_ROOT}/src`.

- T1 `band(480, 0, 0, 0, 5, 120)` is `{5, 5, 470, 120}`; viewable insets shift and narrow it the
  way the launcher's `left`/`right` do.
- T2 `CoverBandGeometry::thumbHeightFor(band.width, band.height) == 783`, equal to the launcher Bible
  tile's (`thumbHeightFor(470, h)` for any tile height `h ≤ 783`), so the thumbnail is shared (A1).
- T3 `plate` is the band's bottom `headerHeight + 1` rows and lies inside it; `plateHeader` is `plate`
  less its top row; `contentTop == band.y + band.height`.
- T4 At 480 × 800 with Lyra's `topPadding 5`, `verticalSpacing 8` and `mastheadHeight 120`, the grid
  body `800 − contentTop − 8` gives `NumberGrid::geometryFor` 7 × 10 with `cellSizeFor ≥ MIN_CELL`.
  The largest masthead that keeps 10 rows is ≥ 120.

Run as CI does (`.github/workflows/ci.yml:144-151`): `cmake -S test -B build/test -G Ninja`,
`cmake --build build/test`, `ctest --test-dir build/test -R Masthead --output-on-failure`. The
failing test is written first, then the header.

**Build and format:** `pio run` (x4pro), then `./bin/clang-format-fix` over the whole tree after `git add`.

**Device (flag for the owner):**

1. Grid, Meetings, Publications: the band shows the right cover, the plate is solid white with the
   title and battery, and no text sits over dither.
2. The grid is still 7 × 10. `LOG_DBG` the body height and confirm cells ≥ 56.
3. Selection moves on all three stay FAST with no ghosting. Meetings and Publications enter with one
   HALF (`Paint`/mode visible in the log), the grid enters FAST, and Chapter → Book takes one HALF
   with no masthead ghost.
4. The heap lines appear on entry to each screen. Internal free stays above ~50 KB and does not
   fall across an enter/exit cycle.
5. The `Masthead` draw-time log. If a selection move is visibly slow, open the PSRAM-cache follow-up
   (A9).
6. First entry to Meetings and Publications after flashing generates `thumb_783` (and new card
   thumbnails on Meetings, A11) once; the second entry generates nothing.

## Risks

- Leaving a masthead screen for the reader or the launcher is a paint those screens own, not this
  one. The launcher repaints FAST unless `cleanInitialRefresh` is set (`LauncherRefresh.h:15-17`).
  Art-over-art ghosting there is a device check, not a change in this issue.
- `ThemeMetrics` gains a field. The only construction sites are the two designated-initializer
  tables (`BaseTheme.h:127`, `LyraTheme.h:9`); `UITheme` only copies the struct (`UITheme.cpp:65,88`).
  A table that omits `.mastheadHeight` still compiles and reads **0**, which would silently collapse
  the band: `platformio.ini` has no `-Werror`. Both tables must set it explicitly, and `Masthead::bandRect`
  logs an error and treats a non-positive height as "no masthead" (compact header, content at today's
  top) rather than drawing a zero-height band.
